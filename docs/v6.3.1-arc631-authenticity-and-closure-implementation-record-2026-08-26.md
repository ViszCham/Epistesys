### 総合結果

ARC631は、v6.3.1の強状態を「public fieldの見た目」から切り離し、principal、receipt class、subject revision、scope、nonce、payload digest、発行時刻、失効時刻、parent receiptへ束縛した。wire envelopeはdeserialize後に必ずUntrustedへ戻り、exact policyとReplayGuardを通過した`VerifiedReceipt`だけがAuthority、Closure、Host、RPA、TLDG、GPU、Mediaのstrong gateへ入る。

公開JSONの既存fieldは削除・renameしていない。additive fieldと新schemaを追加し、旧`VerifiedHostUserSpan`、`HostBound`、`ValidatedObservation`、`promotion_allowed`、`release_complete`相当のbool／enumはshadow DTOとして保持しながら、新gateでは拒否する。

### Overall result

ARC631 separates v6.3.1 strong states from the appearance of public fields and binds them to principal, receipt class, subject revision, scope, nonce, payload digest, issue time, expiry, and parent receipt. A wire envelope always returns to Untrusted after deserialization. Only a `VerifiedReceipt` produced by an exact policy and ReplayGuard can enter Authority, Closure, Host, RPA, TLDG, GPU, or Media strong gates.

No existing public JSON field was deleted or renamed. Additive fields and schemas were introduced. Legacy `VerifiedHostUserSpan`, `HostBound`, `ValidatedObservation`, `promotion_allowed`, and `release_complete`-like booleans and enums remain shadow DTOs but are rejected by new strong gates.

### Implemented connections

1. Added `lc631-receipt-kernel` with HMAC-SHA-256, constant-time comparison, random nonces, expiry, exact scope, and replay rejection.
2. Legacy constructors for `AuthorityEvent` and `ClosureWitness` cannot create strong states and require opaque verified wrappers.
3. Host seeds reject arbitrary nonempty strings, and candidate output digests are recomputed from text.
4. Host-output attestations are re-verified at the durable-replay sink; the v2 ledger includes the authenticity receipt digest in its hash chain. V1 ledgers are read-compatible only.
5. RPA validation, stage completion, evaluation, v6.3.0 baseline, host output, package, and remote CI use separate receipt classes.
6. RPA separates `EvidenceState` from `ClosureState`; Observed with a blocker is not Satisfied.
7. ProjectionDefectGraph v3 retains SourceLedgerId, obligation anchors, ProjectionEdgeId, revision-bound TargetClaimId, and stage paths.
8. IntroducedWithoutSource remains a target-only edge and cannot impersonate a Must obligation.
9. A Must cannot reach CandidateCommit until all four output stages or authenticated NotApplicable receipts exist.
10. RPA and Media domain details connect to CanonicalTranslationEnvelope without automatic scalar compression.
11. TLDG builtin backends start at RevisionPinned and cannot become Validated without execution receipts. The legacy boolean release API always fails closed.
12. NumericBackend is sealed, and the broker checks declared kind against raw kind.
13. Unknown, NotApplicable, and Incomparable remain distinct in masks and relations. WorldContradiction counts only Conflict, while Incomparability has a separate lane.
14. The weak-summary collision fixture `[0,0,2,1]` / `[0,1,0,2]` is separated. Parity compares context, semantic binding, row identity, masks, summaries, finalist values, cutoff, and ties.
15. 256 worlds by eight projections materialize as 2,048 `WorldEvaluation` rows whose projection effects and numeric identities reach GPU input.
16. Relations use a bounded sparse plan per row instead of all 4.2 million pairs and connect to device-side chunk reduction.
17. Distillation performs real `max_epochs` iterations and retains epoch input/output revisions plus ReprojectionRequest without implicit grammar or threshold mutation.
18. Tool processes retain resolved executable path/hash, cwd digest, sanitized environment digest, network-isolation gaps, pipe-drain state, and process-tree-kill receipts.
19. Media source authority cannot arise from a rights boolean or existing path; it requires a source-snapshot receipt.
20. Media execution binds Python, script, FFmpeg, model manifest, license, method IDs, configuration, and resource budgets.
21. HOG, Haar, frame difference, acoustic clustering, and temporal co-presence are separated from inflated method labels, and script output remains ExecutedCandidate.
22. Media observation digests rebind source fragments, backend, model, configuration, and raw candidates, producing Media-to-TL edges.
23. Added legacy strong-state quarantine and a 22-case adversarial taxonomy fixture.
24. Aligned additive schemas, packaged binary, plugin identity, and README/document revision boundaries.
